# Design choices and where to see them

Every choice the Tenancy pages describe, with its **code**, something to **try** on the running
[sample](../the-sample.md), and a **test** that fails when it is undone.

- **Try it**: a preset of the UI's Try it page, by its id in
  [`DevAttempts.cs`](../../../Examples/Tenancy/Examples.Tenancy.Host/DevLogin/DevAttempts.cs); a UI page; a request
  in the host's [`.http` file](../../../Examples/Tenancy/Examples.Tenancy.Host/Examples.Tenancy.Host.http), marked
  (`.http`) and found under who sends it; or a command. [Try it](try-it.md) starts the sample.
- **Test**: a test class under `Tests/`. The sample's are in `Tests/Examples.Tenancy.Tests`; one that needs the host
  with a database takes it from the fixture `SampleHosts` and needs Docker.
